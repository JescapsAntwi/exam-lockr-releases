# Faculty and invigilator guide

## Before the exam

**Create the exam** from the faculty dashboard, then set an access code —
students need it to start, and you can read it any time by expanding the exam
row.

**Check the roster.** Open an exam's **Students** list to see everyone
registered for it. `allowedStudents` is fixed when the exam is created, so
enrol students first, then create the exam.

**Grant accommodations** for anyone who needs them. Two different things:

- **Add extra time** — minutes added on top of whatever the student has left.
- **Set total time** — the student's clock becomes exactly this, ignoring the
  exam duration. Use this for a make-up window.

Getting this wrong is the difference between a student having ten minutes and
having twenty-five.

## Running the exam

**Start** the exam when everyone is ready. The countdown begins from that
moment, not the scheduled time.

**Add time** gives every student more minutes, on top of any individual
accommodation.

**Pause** stops every student's clock. Nobody loses time while paused — use it
for a fire alarm, a power cut, or a projector failure. **Resume** restarts it.

**End** closes the exam for everyone. Every attempt in progress is submitted
with whatever the student had written, and their screens shut down
immediately.

**Ending one student** is separate: use the **End** action beside their name in
the roster. The exam keeps running for everyone else.

## Watching for problems

The proctor dashboard shows who is active, who is flagged, and any help
requests. Replying to a help request sends the message to that student alone.
Your reply stays visible so you and any colleague can see what was already
said.

The **History** button opens past sittings and the full session record — every
activity-log entry, help request and flag, in the order it happened, with CSV
export.

## Afterwards

**Reopen** an exam to let students attempt it again — either for everyone, or
only for students with a make-up window. Reopening also clears any misconduct
lockout it supersedes, so a student flagged in error can sit it again.

A reopened student keeps the work they had already done, and their time is
whatever they had left plus anything you grant.

**Publish results** and **release review** are separate toggles. Results show
the score; review shows the questions and the student's own answers.

## Known limits

- The apps are not yet code-signed, so students see a warning on first run.
  The troubleshooting page tells them what to click.
- Linux lockdown is the weakest of the three platforms. Run Linux students
  under invigilation.
